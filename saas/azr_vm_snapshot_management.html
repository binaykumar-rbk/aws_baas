Management of Azure virtual machine snapshots

You can perform management tasks for the snapshots of Azure virtual machines and managed disks. This includes taking on-demand snapshots, changing the retention policies, and deleting the snapshots.

Efficient management of snapshots of Azure virtual machines and managed disks is important for maintaining data integrity, ensuring quick recovery, and adhering to compliance requirements. On-demand snapshots provide a mechanism to capture the precise state of workloads prior to executing modifications, thereby protecting against potential data loss. Adjusting retention policies and deleting outdated snapshots contributes to optimized storage utilization and cost efficiency.

RSC allows you to perform the following management tasks for the snapshots of Azure virtual machines and managed disks:
  • Taking on-demand snapshots of the workloads when required
  • Changing the retention policy for on-demand snapshots
  • Changing the retention policy for the snapshots taken according to the policies configured in the assigned SLA Domain
  • Deleting an on-demand snapshot
  • Viewing details of the Azure subscriptions and resource groups
