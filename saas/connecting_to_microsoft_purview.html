Connecting to Microsoft Purview

You can connect to Microsoft Purview to detect and assign Microsoft Information Protection labels that do not match data sensitivity findings in RSC. When configured, Rubrik will have visibility into the labels applied to your documents, and you can define your own policies to identify mislabeled and unlabeled sensitive data.

Before you begin

Configure a Microsoft Purview account where you can assign Rubrik access permissions. Log in as a global administrator in RSC to authorize access.

Procedure

  1. Log in to RSC.
  2. Click and select Settings.
    The Settings menu appears.
  3. Click Integrations.
    The Integrations page appears.
  4. On the Microsoft Purview integration card, click Connect.
    The Microsoft Purview page appears.
  5. On the Microsoft Purview page, click Add Integration.
    The Connect to Microsoft Purview dialog box appears.
  6. In Subscription, select a subscription to grant access to.
  7. Click Sign in to Microsoft Purview and complete the integration set up.
  8. Click Finish.
    RSC displays a message that Microsoft Purview was successfully connected.

Results

RSC begins identifying labels assigned to your files, which may take up to an hour to complete.

What to do next

You can now create data security policies to identify where sensitive data is mislabeled or discover missing labels on files.
