Removing M365 apps

Remove a Microsoft 365 app when you no longer need it.

Before you begin

You cannot delete the last app for a product that is associated with an active organization. RSC requires at least one app per product, such as Exchange, OneDrive, Teams, or SharePoint, for each organization

Procedure

  1. Log in to RSC.
  2. Click and select Data Protection.
  3. On the navigation bar, click Inventory.
  4. On the Inventory page, click the Microsoft 365 inventory card.
  5. Click Manage Enterprise Apps.
  6. Locate the app to remove, click , and then select Remove App.
  7. In the Remove app dialog box, click Remove.

Results

You have successfully removed the Microsoft 365 app.
